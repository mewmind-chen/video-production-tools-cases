## YouTube Shorts

**标题**

Can you read this reel label? 4 checks before you pay

**描述**

“Label photo attached. 100% original.” Can you read what is on it?

Four checks before paying a broker:

1. Read the fields: 1P is reportedly the part number; Q is quantity, 9D the date code, 1T the lot, and 4L the country of origin.
2. Check the date code: usually year and week. A date after the part's last-time-buy is a red flag.
3. Some Japanese makers use EIAJ C-3. The reel we checked had no separate date code field; match the lot to the certificate of conformance instead.
4. Compare the label with the parts: part number, origin and lot. Watch for barcodes that differ from printed text, or odd fonts and spacing.

These are screening red flags, not proof of counterfeiting. Labels, part numbers, quantities and lot values shown are examples. The Japanese-label observation concerns one reel, not every Japanese label.
