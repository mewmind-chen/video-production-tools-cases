**正文**

> "4,000 pcs in stock. Ships today."
> With MLCC and memory on allocation, that message is either your lifeline or bait.
>
> 5 checks before you pay a broker:
> 1. A photo taken today, with the date and your company name next to the parts
> 2. Date codes that match across reels and make sense for the part
> 3. Label data (P/N, QTY, LOT) that matches what the manufacturer issued
> 4. A Certificate of Conformance and purchase history that match the reel
> 5. A yes to third-party testing (X-ray, inspection, decap for critical parts)
>
> A seller with real stock says yes to all five.

**结尾问题**

> Which check has saved you the most money? Or which one did a broker refuse?
