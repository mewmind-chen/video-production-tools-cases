**标题**：The rare-earth deadline moved to Jan 10. Only two months. #shorts

**描述**：

Nov 10 is no longer the date. Jan 10, 2027 is — four weeks before Chinese New Year.

• Sep 28: Beijing confirmed the US–China truce now runs to Jan 10, 2027
• That truce paused China's Oct 2025 rare-earth rules. The Apr 2025 rules never paused: 7 rare earths still need an export license (up to 45 working days)
• Yttrium is the most common rare-earth dopant in X7R MLCCs; Dy/Tb go into high-temperature magnets
• Nov 27: China's pause on its Ga/Ge/Sb ban for the US ends. No extension announced as of Oct 17
• CNY 2027 = Feb 6. Factories close 1–3 weeks. Experienced importers place last pre-holiday orders in early November

What to do: plan pre-holiday orders now; ask suppliers if any material needs a Chinese export license; if you ship to the US, watch Nov 27.

What we don't know: what happens on Jan 10. Watch APEC Shenzhen, Nov 18–19.
