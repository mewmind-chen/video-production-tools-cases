```
Electronic components · Shenzhen | I translate China's component market for overseas buyers | Market Pulse every Wednesday
```

**简介（About，最多 2,600 字符）**：

```
I work in electronic components in Shenzhen.

Every week I turn China's first-hand supply chain signals into 60-second videos for overseas buyers: Chinese-language news, Huaqiangbei spot quotes, what domestic distributors are saying.

Three shows:
• News Decoder (Mon): one supply chain story, and what it means for your BOM
• Market Pulse (Wed): prices and lead times from China's spot market
• Buyer's Playbook (Fri): a method you can use this week

How I work:
• Every number has a source in the caption
• I don't predict prices. I quote forecasts and say whose they are
• I don't sell in my posts

Human-sourced. AI-narrated. I pick the stories and check every number; an AI voice tells them.

Part you can't find? Send me the part number. I'll tell you what Shenzhen is seeing. Free.
```

**精选（Featured）**，按顺序：

1. 最新一期 Market Pulse
